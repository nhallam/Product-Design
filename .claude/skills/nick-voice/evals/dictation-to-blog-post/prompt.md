New blog post: Okay so this one is about the stickers thing on my website. Basically when I redesigned my site this year I added this easter egg where stickers would appear scattered around the pages, like actual stickers from my life, a record store one, a G train one, that kind of thing. And people really liked finding them. But recently I'm applying for jobs and sending the site to a lot of people and I started worrying that recruiters would open it and see cartoon stickers everywhere and not take the work seriously. So I paused them, there's literally a flag in the code called stickersPaused. And I keep thinking about how that's sort of a sad little compromise, like the stickers are the most me thing on the site and the first thing I did when the stakes went up was hide them. Anyway I don't have a neat conclusion, maybe the conclusion is that I'll turn them back on when I've signed a contract. Something about personal websites being allowed to be personal.
